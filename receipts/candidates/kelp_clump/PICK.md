# kelp_clump: picked A (primitives + strips), 1,860 tris, verify ok, 1.25 x 0.73 x 1.48 m
A won by eye: a jittered icosahedron stone with ribbon kelp as bent flat DoubleSide strips that arch off the top, drape down one side and lie out on the ground toward front-left, as in the ref, plus forked bladderwrack fronds (paired oval bladders) standing on the back-right of the stone. Two strand colours (kelp green, brown-olive).
B (each strand a thin rectangle extruded along the drape path, 2,560) has real strand thickness but the Frenet frame twists the strands into curls and it costs more. C (radial mop, 1,584) reads the same from every side but loses the ref's flowing one-sided silhouette and reads as a wig on a rock.
Known weakness: strands crossing the stone's steep side hang as a curtain rather than hugging it; the bladders are faceted octahedra and read as small leaves past a few metres; footprint is diagonal (1.25 x 1.48) rather than 1.4 square. Stone height 0.43 m, bladderwrack to 0.73 m (brief said 0.8).
