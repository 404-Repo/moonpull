# cockle_spade: B (extruded blade outline + lathe socket/shaft + extruded D handle), 640 tris
Verify: ok, 0.19 x 0.096 x 0.976 m against 0.9 m (tolerance 0.1), 3/3 clean at --size=560.
Won by eye: the extruded outline gives a clean square-shouldered blade with a rounded nose and a bright worn edge, and the D handle makes it read as a spade rather than a shovel or a stick. A (primitives) has a pan-like disc tip and an orange rust dot that reads as a sticker. C (dished graft blade, Y handle) is the most interesting shape but the Y handle reads as a fork.
Known weakness: the iron is dark, so at 11 m on wet sand it reads mostly as the pale shaft with a dark end. 8% over the 0.9 m length.
