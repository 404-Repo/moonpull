# stilt_house_b: picked A (11,952 tris, verify clean, 5.9 x 9.5 x 5.4 m)

A won because its cobbled chimney (individual bevelled stones) and its walls split around real openings (glass and doors recessed in the wall thickness) read closest to the reference from all four sides. After round one it also took C's sagging roof and gained the hanging net. B's extruded chimney courses read as a stack of tyres, and its notched tile strips looked flat. C's ashlar chimney with a shoulder is a different stone type from the reference's cobbles, and its openings are projecting casings, not recesses.
